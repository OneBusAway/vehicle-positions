Rider mode is on

Riders pick their trip in a rider app, and their phones report where they are. Each position is checked against the schedule and the route's shape. A trusted feed that puts its own vehicle in the same place confirms the rider. A vehicle goes in the feed from riders only when one trusted rider, or two riders who agree, are on it, and never when a trusted feed or one of your drivers already reports that trip.
